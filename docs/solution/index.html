Four Outcomes, One Deterministic Control Set

Every engagement is scoped against these four liabilities, not just against threats

Company Risk

Insurance-Grade Protection

Insurers are increasingly voiding claims where attested controls, such as "organisation-wide MFA," cannot be independently verified at the time of loss. We build to the standard underwriters recognise and negotiate directly with them for improved terms, so your policy pays out when it matters.

Personal Risk

Director Defensibility

Section 180 exposure is personal, and it survives whatever coverage the company carries. We align your control environment to SMB1001 Level 3 (Gold) — and exceed it in the areas examined first under forensic review — so individual directors have an independently verifiable standard of care to point to.

Business Risk

Operational Continuity

Prevention will eventually fail once; containment cannot be allowed to fail alongside it. We architect around minimal blast radius, so a single compromised credential or device is a contained incident, not a firm-wide outage or a notifiable data breach.

Evidentiary Risk

Proof of Care

A control that cannot be evidenced at a specific point in time is, forensically and legally, indistinguishable from a control that did not exist. We issue timestamped, cryptographically signed reports on control status, on a regular cadence — a contemporaneous record your insurer, regulator, or counsel can rely on without taking your word for it.

What This Looks Like In Practice

■

No Passwords Left to Steal

Passwordless authentication and passkeys are enforced organisation-wide, removing the single most common vector in credential-based breaches — not offered as an opt-in for staff who choose to use it.

■

Zero Standing Access

No individual — including us — holds permanent administrative rights. Elevated access is granted just-in-time, scoped to the task, and automatically revoked and logged.

■

Deterministic Access Policy

Conditional Access policies deny any authentication attempt that does not meet policy, by default. This is not a monitored exception queue — non-compliant access is structurally impossible, not merely flagged.

■

Application Control by Default

Only explicitly approved software is permitted to execute. Ransomware payloads and unauthorised tooling are blocked at execution, rather than detected afterward by an analyst reviewing alerts.

■

Immutable, Segmented Backups

Backups are unreachable and unalterable from a compromised account, including a compromised administrator account, and systems are segmented so no single failure point can cascade firm-wide.

■

Signed, Timestamped Reporting

Control status is reported on a regular cadence and cryptographically signed at time of issue, producing a tamper-evident record rather than a retrospective, self-assessed attestation.

Insurer & Regulator Baseline

We deploy to Maturity Level 1 as a floor, not a ceiling, because it remains the reference point underwriters and regulators default to when assessing whether reasonable controls were in place.

SMB1001 Level 3 (Gold)

Director-Grade Standard of Care

Purpose-built for organisations of your size, SMB1001 Gold gives individual directors an independently published, third-party standard to point to — rather than an internal policy of the board's own making.

We exceed Gold specifically in passkey enforcement, application control, and zero standing admin access — the control areas examined first in a post-incident forensic review.
