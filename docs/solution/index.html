Framework Alignment

Deterministic controls, mapped against the standards insurers, regulators, and forensic examiners actually apply

Essential Eight

Insurer & Regulator Baseline

We deploy to Maturity Level 1 as a floor, not a ceiling, because it remains the reference point underwriters and regulators default to when assessing whether reasonable controls were in place.

Essential Eight remains an important Australian baseline. We align its controls with broader ISM guidance and additional safeguards appropriate to each organisation’s risks, systems and obligations.

SMB1001 Level 3 (Gold)

Director-Grade Standard of Care

Purpose-built for organisations of your size, SMB1001 Gold gives individual directors an independently published, third-party standard to point to rather than an internal policy of the board's own making.

We exceed Gold specifically in passkey enforcement, application control, and zero standing admin access the control areas examined first in a post-incident forensic review.

Five Controls. One Body of Evidence.

Each control closes a specific, common path to a breach. VeriState reporting then shows you, and anyone who asks, that each one is working.

1

Phishing resistant Authentication

No passwords left to steal.

Staff sign in with passkeys tied to their device, so there is no password to phish, reuse or leak.

Outcome: Removes one of the most common causes of account compromise.

2

Protected Administration

No permanent administrator access.

Admin rights are granted for an approved task and removed automatically when the task is done.

Outcome: Limits the damage any single compromised account can do.

3

Session Protection

Non-compliant access is blocked, not flagged.

Every sign-in is checked against policy for device, location and risk. If it doesn't meet policy, access is denied rather than raising an alert for someone to review later. Browser sessions are hardened against token theft.

Outcome: Consistent enforcement, with no quiet exceptions.

4

Execution Control

Only approved software runs.

Applications are explicitly allowed. Anything not on the list is blocked before it runs, including the unapproved programs ransomware commonly relies on.

Outcome: Closes off one of the most common ways ransomware takes hold.

5

Immutable Recovery

Backups cannot be modified, deleted or encrypted by attackers

Backups are stored so they can't be altered or deleted during their retention period, and recovery is tested against agreed recovery objectives.

Outcome: A known, tested path back after an incident.

Veristate™ Assurance Reporting

Evidence, not opinions.

Signed, timestamped reports showing the state of each control at the time it was checked, delivered to you monthly.

Outcome: Evidence you can hand to auditors, insurers, clients and your board.

Four Outcomes, One Deterministic Control Set

Every engagement is scoped against these four liabilities, not just against threats

Company RiskInsurance-Grade Protection

Cyber insurance applications commonly require organisations to confirm that controls such as MFA, endpoint protection and protected backups are in place. We independently verify those controls, maintain evidence of their operation and help your organisation provide accurate information to its broker or insurer. This supports more informed underwriting and reduces the risk of a gap between what was declared and what was operating at the time of an incident.

Personal RiskDirector Defensibility

Section 180 exposure is personal, and it survives whatever coverage the company carries. We align your control environment to SMB1001 Level 3 (Gold) and exceed it in the areas examined first under forensic review so individual directors have an independently verifiable standard of care to point to.

Business RiskOperational Continuity

Prevention will eventually fail once; containment cannot be allowed to fail alongside it. We architect around minimal blast radius, so a single compromised credential or device is a contained incident, not a firm-wide outage or a notifiable data breach.

Evidentiary RiskProof of Care

A control that cannot be evidenced at a specific point in time is, forensically and legally, indistinguishable from a control that did not exist. We issue timestamped, cryptographically signed reports on control status, on a regular cadence a contemporaneous record your insurer, regulator, or counsel can rely on without taking your word for it.
