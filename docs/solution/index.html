Cybersecurity Solution

Intuitive & Verifiable

Each control is built around a simple idea you can picture: only you can sign in as you, only approved apps can run, nobody holds the keys all the time, and your backups sit where a thief can’t reach them.

Real protection is rarely a single toggle. Every control is actually a chain of safeguards working in sequence, mapped line-by-line to proven standards like the ASD Essentials, SMB1001, and MITRE ATT&CK. We connect those pieces so you don't have to.

The result is protection you can understand at a glance. Straightforward concepts that pass or fail, leaving zero guesswork about where you stand.

Phishing-Resistance

VS.DA.01

Every user, with no exceptions, signs in with a phishing-resistant credential. Passkeys are defined, assigned and enforced, so credential theft, password phishing and MFA fatigue become things of the past.

Users who haven't registered a passkey yet don't cause a failure, because enforcement means they can't sign in until they do.

ASD Essentials principles

  • PRO-13Identity, credential and access management (2 actions)

SMB1001:2026 controls

  • SilverMFA on employee email (Full)
  • GoldMFA on business apps and social media (Full)
  • PlatinumMFA where important data is stored (Full)
  • BronzeStrong password hygiene (Full)
  • SilverIndividual user accounts (Supports)
  • SilverPassword manager (Partial)

ATT&CK techniques it helps mitigate

Account ManipulationAdversary-in-the-MiddleBrute ForceMulti-Factor Authentication Request GenerationPhishingRemote ServicesValid Accounts

Advanced Attack Defense

VS.EC.05

Adversaries love to use your own system against you. Vulnerable drivers are blocked, standard utilities can’t be combined into attack tools (so called “living off the land”), stored credentials are shielded from theft, and legacy protocols such as SMBv1, NTLM and LLMNR are switched off. Even extremely capable attackers struggle to make a home in your environment.
