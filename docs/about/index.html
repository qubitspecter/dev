About Decoheron

Security isn't a product you install.

Too many organisations do the minimum and expect the maximum from their security. They buy the right products, install them, receive a report, and assume they're protected.

We've seen this from both sides of the table, as customers buying security and as managed service providers delivering it. The tools were rarely the problem. The problem was whether they were configured properly, whether anyone checked they were actually working, and whether the people responsible understood the threats those tools were meant to stop.

Decoheron exists to close that gap. We take the security you've already paid for, make it work properly, and prove it.

  • Alongside your MSP. We strengthen the environment your MSP runs, rather than competing for it.
