Why Decoheron

Why another security company?

Small regulated businesses are already hopelessly behind the threat landscape, and the gap is about to widen. Law firms, accountants and wealth managers hold some of the most sensitive information in their clients’ lives, with a fraction of the defences. I’ll spare you the stats and the charts, but AI is rapidly changing the manner, accessibility and speed of cyberattacks. Many of these firms will simply not survive it.

I came to this from the defence sector, where you learn that you don’t win by watching an attacker more closely. You win by building systems where the attack can’t work. Then I looked at small business and saw the opposite: security sold as monitoring and alerts, an ongoing guess about whether someone is already inside. Most firms I look at can’t even get their email authentication right. That isn’t negligence. They were sold the wrong thing.

That is why we started Decoheron. The name is the idea. In quantum physics, decoherence is where something uncertain settles into one definite state, and that is what we want for security: not “probably fine”, but enforced or not, and provable either way. Tom and I built this to do exactly that. I handle the security, he makes sure it lands smoothly in a business that can’t afford disruption, and then we get out of the way.

The founders

Robert Shea

Founder & Technical Partner

Robert brings 30 years of security experience across battlefield systems, high-assurance computing and enterprise defence. In those environments, a control is either proven to work or treated as absent. He brings that same discipline to every Decoheron engagement.
