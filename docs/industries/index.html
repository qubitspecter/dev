Who We Work With

The Obligation is Different.
The Problem is the Same.

Every organisation we work with arrives with a different trigger. A licence condition. A new AUSTRAC obligation. A client's security schedule. A renewal questionnaire that asked a question nobody could answer honestly.

Underneath all of them sits one problem: the organisation has controls it believes are working, and no way to prove it. That is the problem we solve, and it does not change with your industry.

Industries We Work In

Find the regime that applies to your business.

Section 912A · CPS 234 · CPS 230

Financial Services & Wealth

AFS licensees, insurers, superannuation funds, and advice networks. Licence obligations to maintain adequate risk management systems and adequate technological resources, with APRA-regulated entities carrying CPS 234 and CPS 230 on top. This is the sector where the standard of adequacy has now been tested in court, which makes it the sector where the expectations are clearest.

AML/CTF Tranche 2

Legal & Professional Services

Law firms, accounting practices, and conveyancers captured by AML/CTF Tranche 2 since July 2026, now required to hold seven years of customer identification and source of funds records. Firms already holding privileged client material, transaction data, and trust account details, with an obligation to protect it that has just become statutory rather than professional.

AML/CTF Tranche 2 · Payment Fraud

Property & Real Estate

Agencies, developers, and property groups captured by Tranche 2, handling identity documents and settlement funds through processes designed for speed rather than security. Invoice fraud and payment redirection remain the dominant loss mechanism in this sector, and both are email compromise problems before they are financial ones.
