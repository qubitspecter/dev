Five controls. One body of evidence.

Each control closes a specific, common path to a breach. VeriState™ reporting then shows you, and anyone who asks, that each one is working.

1

Phishing resistant Authentication

No passwords left to steal.

Staff sign in with passkeys tied to their device, so there is no password to phish, reuse or leak.

Outcome: Removes one of the most common causes of account compromise.

2

Protected Administration

No permanent administrator access.

Admin rights are granted for an approved task and removed automatically when the task is done.

Outcome: Limits the damage any single compromised account can do.

3

Session Protection

Non-compliant access is blocked, not flagged.

Every sign-in is checked against policy for device, location and risk. If it doesn't meet policy, access is denied rather than raising an alert for someone to review later. Browser sessions are hardened against token theft.

Outcome: Consistent enforcement, with no quiet exceptions.

4

Execution Control

Only approved software runs.

Applications are explicitly allowed. Anything not on the list is blocked before it runs, including the unapproved programs ransomware commonly relies on.

Outcome: Closes off one of the most common ways ransomware takes hold.

5

Immutable Recovery

Backups cannot be modified, deleted or encrypted by attackers

Backups are stored so they can't be altered or deleted during their retention period, and recovery is tested against agreed recovery objectives.

Outcome: A known, tested path back after an incident.

Veristate™ Assurance Reporting

Evidence, not opinions.

Signed, timestamped reports showing the state of each control at the time it was checked, delivered to you monthly.

Outcome: Evidence you can hand to auditors, insurers, clients and your board.
