1. What happened

Attackers deployed off-the-shelf ransomware. The firm's data was backed up, but its operating environment wasn't, so recovery took days rather than hours.

Security at the time rested on basic malware monitoring from the firm's outsourced IT provider. A client who knew Decoheron's founder from earlier work suggested the firm engage us for an Essential Eight uplift. The ASD Essential Eight is the Australian Signals Directorate's set of eight baseline strategies for preventing and limiting cyber security incidents.

2. What we found

Forensics never established exactly how the attackers got in. It didn't need to. Any one of these would have made their job easier:

  • Seven Global Admin accounts (the highest-privilege role in Microsoft 365, able to change any setting), five of them used daily for browsing and email.
  • Most staff had administrator rights on their own devices.
  • Multi-factor authentication was often enabled, but not enforced.
  • No devices were centrally managed, and personal devices could sign in freely.
  • Backups covered data, but not the operating environment.

4. What changed

For the partners

Cyber insurance cover went up and the annual premium came down. IT running costs fell too, thanks to clear documentation and fewer alerts. With daily VeriState verification, the partners know their security position on any given day, rather than hoping.

For clients

Several clients have since asked about the firm's security. The firm now answers with two reports, available within 24 hours: one showing how it addresses each Essential Eight strategy, and one verifying the current state of the environment, so any drift from the baseline shows up straight away.

For staff

With devices managed, application control in place and no one holding standing admin rights, day-to-day IT is smoother and problems from unexpected changes have stopped. Mobile access is limited to Teams and email. Because sign-in is passwordless, staff training now focuses on payment fraud instead of password phishing.
