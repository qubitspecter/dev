1. Why the firm acted

Every Victorian law practice is expected to meet the VLSB+C's minimum cyber security expectations. This firm didn't want to stop there. Client expectations were rising, and it wanted to be ready for new risks rather than react to them.

It was also planning to grow, but had no clear view of its security posture and was concerned that security wouldn't hold up through that change. It asked Decoheron for an approach aligned with three frameworks: the ASD Essential Eight, SMB1001 (a tiered cyber security standard for small business) and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF).

2. What we found

  • Controls were configured, not enforced. Multi-factor authentication, application control and Conditional Access were in place, but several were in audit or report-only mode. They logged threats instead of stopping them.
  • Meeting the minimum wasn't proving anything. Basic security broadly met the VLSB+C expectations, but didn't demonstrate alignment with any recognised framework.
  • There was no strategy. Without a clear view of its posture, the firm's growth plans were held back by uncertainty about whether security would survive the change.

4. What changed

For the partners

The firm runs one security program instead of four. One control set covers governance, identity, privilege, applications, procedures and hardening across every framework it cares about. As it expands into jurisdictions where SMB1001 Gold is already recommended, it's positioned to respond quickly.

For clients

When a client or insurer asks how the firm protects their information, it can provide evidence of its current security posture within 24 hours through Veristate. Because its controls are already mapped to the Essential Eight, SMB1001 and NIST CSF, new client requirements are a mapping exercise, not a new project.

For growth

Devices deploy zero-touch, meaning they're ready to use on first sign-in with no manual setup. Personal mobiles are supported securely, with access limited to email and Teams. That's what let the firm open its satellite office quickly, on the same enforced controls as head office. It also now has a security strategy with defined goals for continual improvement.
